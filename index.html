Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about going solar in Brisbane and Queensland

Solar system prices in Brisbane range from $3,500 to $15,000+ after the STC rebate, depending on system size and component quality. A popular 6.6kW solar system typically costs between $4,000-$6,000 fully installed, while a larger 10kW system ranges from $7,000-$10,000. SolarYoot offers competitive pricing with flexible payment plans to make solar accessible for every Brisbane household.
Queensland residents can access the federal Small-scale Technology Certificates (STC) rebate, which can save you $2,000-$4,000 on your solar system depending on size and location. Queensland also offers competitive solar feed-in tariffs for excess energy you export back to the grid. SolarYoot handles all the rebate paperwork for you, ensuring you receive the maximum discount on your solar installation.
Most residential solar panel installations in Brisbane and the Sunshine Coast are completed in a single day, typically taking 4-8 hours. The full process from initial consultation to installation usually takes 2-4 weeks, including custom system design, council approvals, and scheduling. Our CEC accredited installers work efficiently to minimise disruption to your home.
The ideal solar system size depends on your energy consumption, available roof space, and budget. Most Australian homes benefit from a 6.6kW to 10kW system. A small household with $200-$300 quarterly bills may suit a 6.6kW system, while larger families typically need 10kW or more. Our free solar consultation analyses your electricity bills and recommends the optimal system size for maximum savings.
Yes, solar panels still generate electricity on cloudy and overcast days, although at reduced efficiency of around 10-25% of peak output. Brisbane and the Sunshine Coast enjoy some of Australia's highest sunshine hours, meaning your solar panels will perform exceptionally well year-round. Modern Tier-1 panels from JinKo, Trina, and Aiko are designed to perform well even in low-light conditions.
SolarYoot provides a comprehensive 10-year workmanship warranty on all solar installations, covering labour and installation quality. Our Tier-1 solar panels come with 25-year performance warranties from manufacturers like JinKo, Trina Solar, and Aiko. Inverters carry 5-12 year manufacturer warranties from brands such as Sungrow, GoodWe, and Fronius. We also offer extended warranty options and ongoing maintenance plans for complete peace of mind.
