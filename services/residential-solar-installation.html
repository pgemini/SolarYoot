Why Brisbane Homes Need Solar

Queensland's electricity prices have increased by over 120% in the past decade, and they continue to rise. Meanwhile, solar panel prices have dropped significantly, making solar energy more accessible than ever. Here's why now is the perfect time to install solar on your Brisbane home:

  • Slash electricity bills by 50-70% — A well-designed solar system can dramatically reduce your quarterly power bills from $600+ down to under $100.
  • Government solar rebates — Queensland homeowners can access federal STC rebates worth $2,000-$4,000, significantly reducing your upfront cost.
  • Increase property value — Studies show Australian homes with solar panels sell for 3-4% more on average, adding tens of thousands to your home's value.
  • Energy independence — With battery storage, you can store excess solar energy for use at night and during peak tariff periods, reducing grid dependence.
  • Environmental impact — A typical 6.6kW residential solar system prevents approximately 9 tonnes of CO2 emissions per year, equivalent to planting 250 trees.
  • Feed-in tariff income — Export excess solar electricity to the grid and earn credits on your electricity bill through Queensland's solar feed-in tariff scheme.

Our Residential Solar Installation Process

We've refined our installation process over hundreds of projects to ensure every Brisbane homeowner receives a seamless, hassle-free solar experience from first contact to final connection.

Step 1: Free In-Home Consultation

Our solar experts visit your home to assess your roof, inspect your switchboard, analyse your electricity bills, discuss your energy goals, and recommend the optimal solar system. This consultation is completely free with no obligation.

Step 2: Custom System Design

Using advanced solar modelling software and satellite imagery, our engineers design a system optimised for your specific roof layout, orientation, tilt angle, and shading conditions. We calculate expected energy production, savings projections, and payback period.

Step 3: Approvals & Paperwork

We handle everything: grid connection applications, council permits (if required), STC rebate paperwork, and retailer notifications. You don't have to worry about any of the bureaucracy — we take care of it all.

Step 4: Professional Installation

Our CEC accredited installation team arrives on the scheduled day and typically completes the full installation in 4-8 hours. We use premium mounting systems, high-quality cabling, and follow all Australian Standards for electrical and rooftop work.

Step 5: Connection & Monitoring

Once installed, we configure your inverter, connect your system to the grid, set up smart monitoring on your phone, and walk you through how to track your solar production and savings in real time. Start saving from day one.

Residential Solar System Sizes

Choosing the right system size is crucial for maximising your solar investment. Here's our guide based on typical Brisbane household energy usage:

System Size Daily Output* Best For Annual Savings
5kW ~20 kWh 1-2 person household, low usage $1,200 - $1,600
6.6kW ~26 kWh Average 2-3 person household $1,600 - $2,100
8kW ~32 kWh 3-4 person family home $2,000 - $2,600
10kW ~40 kWh Large family, pool, AC, EV charger $2,500 - $3,200
13.3kW ~53 kWh Maximum savings, large roof, battery $3,200 - $4,200

*Average daily output based on Brisbane's solar irradiance data. Actual production varies by season, roof orientation, and shading.
