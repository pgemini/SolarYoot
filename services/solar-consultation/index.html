What Happens During a Solar Consultation

Our solar consultation is a comprehensive, multi-step assessment designed to give you all the information you need to make a confident decision. Here is exactly what our Brisbane solar consultants cover during each visit:

Roof Assessment

We inspect your roof type, material, condition, orientation, and tilt angle to determine the optimal panel placement. Our consultants check for structural integrity, available roof space, and any potential obstacles such as vents, skylights, or antenna mounts that could affect installation. We assess whether your roof faces north, east, or west and calculate expected solar generation for each section.

Electricity Bill Analysis

We review your recent electricity bills in detail to understand your usage patterns, peak consumption times, current tariff structure, and seasonal variations. This analysis reveals exactly how much solar energy your household needs and helps us size your system accurately — ensuring you are not paying for more capacity than you need, or underinvesting and missing out on savings.

Shading Analysis

Shading from trees, neighbouring buildings, or rooftop structures can significantly reduce solar output. We perform a thorough shading analysis using specialist tools to map shade patterns across your roof throughout the day and across seasons. This ensures every panel is positioned for maximum sun exposure and that your system performance projections are realistic and accurate.

Personalised System Recommendation

Based on our assessment findings, we present you with a customised solar system recommendation including the ideal system size, panel and inverter options, expected daily and annual energy production, projected savings, payback period, and return on investment. We also advise on battery storage suitability and future expansion options so your system grows with your needs.

Why Professional Solar Consultation Matters

Many homeowners make the mistake of choosing a solar system based solely on price or a quick online estimate. Without a proper on-site consultation, you risk ending up with a system that underperforms, costs more in the long run, or fails to meet your energy needs. Here is why a professional solar consultation in Brisbane is essential:

  • Avoid oversizing your system — An oversized solar system means you pay more upfront for capacity you will never use. Our consultation ensures you only invest in what your household actually needs, saving you thousands on unnecessary panels and inverter capacity.
  • Avoid undersizing your system — Conversely, a system that is too small will not cover your energy needs, leaving you still paying high electricity bills and extending your payback period. We right-size your system from day one.
  • Maximise your return on investment — By optimising panel placement, selecting the right components, and accurately modelling your energy production, our consultation ensures you achieve the fastest possible payback and highest long-term returns.
  • Identify potential issues early — Our consultants check for roof condition problems, switchboard upgrades needed, shading obstacles, and council or strata requirements before you commit — avoiding costly surprises during installation.
  • Access the right rebates — Queensland solar rebates and Small-scale Technology Certificates (STCs) can save you thousands. Our consultants ensure your system is designed to qualify for all available incentives.
  • Future-proof your investment — We consider your future energy needs including electric vehicles, battery storage, and home expansions so your solar system remains effective for decades to come.

What to Prepare Before Your Consultation

To get the most out of your free solar consultation, we recommend having the following ready:

  • Recent electricity bills — Ideally the last 2-4 quarterly bills so we can analyse your usage patterns across different seasons and identify peak consumption periods.
  • Roof access — Ensure our consultant can safely access your roof or at minimum view it clearly. If you have a multi-storey home, let us know in advance so we can arrange drone inspection equipment.
  • Switchboard location — Know where your main electrical switchboard is located, as this affects cable routing and installation logistics.
  • Energy goals — Think about what you want from solar. Are you primarily looking to reduce bills, achieve energy independence, add battery backup, or prepare for an electric vehicle? This helps us tailor the recommendation.
  • Budget range — Having a rough budget in mind allows us to present realistic options that match your financial expectations while maximising value.
  • Strata or body corporate rules — If you live in a townhouse or unit, check whether there are any restrictions on solar panel installations.

Common Questions Answered During Your Consultation

Our solar consultants are experienced at answering every question Brisbane homeowners have about going solar. Here are some of the most common topics we cover:

  • How much will solar save me? — We provide a detailed savings projection based on your actual usage, local electricity rates, feed-in tariff rates, and your specific system design.
  • What size solar system do I need? — We calculate the optimal system size based on your daily consumption, roof space, budget, and future energy plans.
  • Do I need a battery? — We analyse whether battery storage makes financial sense for your situation based on your self-consumption ratio, time-of-use tariffs, and backup power requirements.
  • How long until the system pays for itself? — We calculate your expected payback period, typically 3-5 years for Brisbane properties, based on realistic production and savings estimates.
  • What rebates am I eligible for? — We explain all available government incentives including STCs, interest-free loan schemes, and any local council rebates applicable to your area.
  • Will solar work on my roof? — We assess roof suitability including orientation, pitch, shading, structural condition, and available space to give you a definitive answer.
  • What happens during a power outage? — We explain the difference between grid-tied and hybrid systems and recommend the right configuration for your needs.
