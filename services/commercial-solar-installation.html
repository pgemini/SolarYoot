Why Your Business Should Go Solar

Businesses across Brisbane and the Sunshine Coast are switching to commercial solar at record rates. Here's why investing in a commercial solar system is one of the smartest financial decisions your business can make:

  • Dramatically reduce electricity costs — Commercial electricity rates in Queensland have reached 28-35c/kWh and continue to climb. A well-sized commercial solar system can offset 50-80% of your electricity consumption, saving your business tens of thousands of dollars every year.
  • Instant tax benefits & depreciation — Under the Australian Tax Office's instant asset write-off provisions, businesses can claim the full cost of a commercial solar system as a tax deduction in the year of purchase. This significantly reduces the effective cost of your solar investment.
  • Federal STC rebates — The Small-scale Renewable Energy Scheme provides substantial upfront rebates for commercial solar systems under 100kW, reducing your out-of-pocket cost by $10,000-$40,000 depending on system size.
  • Boost your green credentials — Customers, tenants, and stakeholders increasingly choose businesses that demonstrate environmental responsibility. Commercial solar showcases your commitment to sustainability and helps meet corporate ESG goals.
  • Hedge against rising energy costs — Lock in your electricity costs for 25+ years. While grid electricity prices continue their upward trajectory, your solar-generated power remains free after the initial investment.
  • Increase property value — Commercial properties with solar installations attract higher rental yields and sale prices. A solar system is a tangible asset that enhances your building's appeal to future buyers or tenants.
  • Feed-in tariff revenue — Export surplus solar electricity to the grid on weekends or holidays when your business is closed and earn feed-in tariff credits on your electricity bill.

Commercial Solar Financing Options

We understand that capital expenditure decisions require careful consideration. SolarYoot offers multiple commercial solar financing options to suit every business's cash flow requirements and financial preferences:

  • Outright Purchase — Pay upfront for your commercial solar system and enjoy the fastest payback, maximum long-term savings, full ownership of the asset, and eligibility for instant asset write-off tax deductions. This option delivers the best overall return on investment.
  • Solar Lease / Chattel Mortgage — Spread the cost of your commercial solar system over 3-7 years with fixed monthly repayments. In many cases, the monthly lease payment is less than the electricity savings from day one, making it a cash-flow-positive investment from the start.
  • Power Purchase Agreement (PPA) — Under a PPA, a third party owns and maintains the solar system on your roof, and you simply purchase the solar electricity at a fixed rate that is lower than your current grid electricity price. Zero upfront cost, zero maintenance responsibility, guaranteed savings.
  • Green Business Loans — Several Australian banks and lenders offer preferential interest rates for green energy investments. We can connect you with finance partners who specialise in commercial solar lending.

Commercial Solar ROI & Payback

Commercial solar delivers some of the strongest returns of any business investment. Here's what Brisbane and Sunshine Coast businesses can typically expect:

  • Payback period: 3-5 years — Most commercial solar systems pay for themselves within 3-5 years through electricity savings alone. After payback, you enjoy essentially free electricity for the remaining 20+ years of the system's life.
  • Internal Rate of Return (IRR): 20-30%+ — Commercial solar regularly delivers IRR figures of 20-30%, far exceeding the returns on most traditional business investments, term deposits, or share portfolios.
  • Total lifetime savings: $200,000 - $1,000,000+ — Over a 25-year system lifespan, commercial solar systems save businesses hundreds of thousands of dollars. Larger systems for warehouses and factories can save well over $1 million.
  • Rising returns over time — As grid electricity prices continue to increase year after year, your solar savings grow proportionally. The value of your commercial solar system actually increases over time.
